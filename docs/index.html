DARE-Arbo

Design, Assay, and Reporting Evaluation for Arboviruses: an endpoint-specific appraisal framework for strengthening the interpretation and synthesis of arbovirus surveillance evidence.

Open the project platformRead the privacy policy
A collaborative methodological study supported by the Global Health and Infectious Diseases Research Group at KCCR and the SYNERGY-NGS-2025 network.
Purpose of the application

Make each reported estimate interpretable

DARE-Arbo links each article-outcome to its target population, biological endpoint, assay pathway, numerator, and denominator. The project platform supports public study information and a controlled collaboration workspace for framework development, pilot appraisal, reliability assessment, and dissemination.

Study design

Appraises surveillance purpose, population representation, sampling-frame coverage, and participant or specimen selection.

Assay design

Examines biological endpoints, diagnostic pathways, assay validity, confirmatory testing, and cross-reactivity.

Outcome reporting

Assesses numerator–denominator compatibility, assay-specific estimates, and article-outcome reconstruction.
