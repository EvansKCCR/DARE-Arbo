Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the DARE-Arbo website and Streamlit project platform use, why it is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to visitors, collaborators, partners, and administrators.

Effective and last updated: 11 November 2026

The DARE-Arbo methodology study evaluates published reports. The platform is not designed to collect clinical records, patient identifiers, or identifiable participant-level research data. Please do not submit such information.

1. Who is responsible for the platform?

DARE-Arbo (Design, Assay, and Reporting Evaluation for Arboviruses) is a collaborative methodological research project coordinated by Evans Asamoah Adu with the Global Health and Infectious Diseases Research Group at the Kumasi Centre for Collaborative Research in Tropical Medicine and project collaborators.

For privacy questions, access requests, corrections, or deletion requests, email evansasamoahadu@gmail.com.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers the public DARE-Arbo information website, the open quality-appraisal tool at dare-arbo-tool.streamlit.app, and the collaborator workspace at dare-arbo-gxsla2u2vugc8gnsjyessi.streamlit.app. It covers public visitors, applicants, approved collaborators, external meeting invitees, team members, and administrators.

Links to the expression-of-interest form, LinkedIn, KCCR, repositories, or other external services are governed by those services’ own privacy terms.

4. Collaborator sign-in and private storage

The quality-appraisal tool is open to visitors and does not require sign-in. The project workspace uses invite-only email one-time-code sign-in. The platform checks the verified email against the administrator-approved collaborator list before granting access to restricted functions.

  • Authentication: Supabase Auth delivers and verifies collaborator one-time codes. The app does not request access to a visitor’s Google or Microsoft account.
  • Private project storage: Supabase Storage holds restricted project documents, the management workbook, meeting records, and private feedback. Server-side access is limited by the application to approved collaborator and administrator functions.

5. Purposes and basis for processing

Information is processed to operate the research collaboration, authenticate approved users, provide requested document and coordination tools, send authorized notifications, respond to feedback, protect the platform, and meet research-governance or legal obligations. Processing is based, as applicable, on the person’s consent and voluntary participation, performance of the requested collaboration functions, and the project’s legitimate interests in secure research administration.

6. Service providers and disclosure

Information may be processed by:

  • Supabase for collaborator email verification and private project storage;
  • the configured SMTP mail provider for project assignment and meeting notifications;
  • Google Forms for the separate expression-of-interest form, if applicants choose to use it;
  • Streamlit Community Cloud / Snowflake for hosting and operating the interactive project platform;
  • GitHub Pages / GitHub for hosting this public information and privacy site;
  • approved DARE-Arbo administrators and collaborators according to their role and the access controls in the platform.

Information may also be disclosed if required by law, to protect rights or security, or during an organizational transition subject to appropriate safeguards. DARE-Arbo does not sell personal information and does not share it with advertising networks.

7. Retention

Project records are retained for as long as needed for active framework development, pilot testing, research documentation, dissemination, audit, and applicable institutional or legal requirements. Authentication sessions expire automatically. Unsuccessful or superseded notification records may be retained to prevent duplicate delivery. Optional feedback contact information and meeting invitee details can be reviewed for deletion when no longer needed. Public team-profile information remains published until the team member or administrator requests an update or removal.

8. Security and international processing

DARE-Arbo uses approved-user checks, verified email one-time codes, administrator-only controls, encrypted HTTPS connections, private secret storage, private-bucket access, file-size and type limits, and version checks before workbook writes. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Service providers may process information in countries other than the user’s country, subject to their contractual and legal safeguards.

9. Your choices and rights

You may browse public project information and use the quality-appraisal tool without signing in. You may choose not to provide optional feedback name or email fields. You can ask to access, correct, restrict, object to, or delete personal information associated with you, subject to applicable research, legal, security, or record-retention requirements. Collaborators may sign out of the workspace and can request removal of their approved access.

External meeting invitees can decline or remove calendar invitations in their own mail/calendar service. Workspace access is controlled separately and is not granted by receiving a meeting invitation.

10. Children

The platform is intended for adult researchers, public-health practitioners, evidence-synthesis teams, and project collaborators. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the platform’s functions, providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised, and material changes will be communicated through the project platform or collaborator channels where appropriate.
