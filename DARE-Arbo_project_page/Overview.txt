DARE-Arbo is a novel endpoint-specific appraisal framework developed to improve the interpretation and synthesis of arbovirus surveillance and seroepidemiological evidence. Recognizing that biologically and methodologically different outcomes should not be treated as equivalent, DARE-Arbo uses the article-outcome as its unit of appraisal and evaluates evidence across the full pathway from target population and biological endpoint to assay design, verification, and outcome reporting. 

By addressing important gaps in existing prevalence, diagnostic, and risk-of-bias assessment tools, DARE-Arbo provides a standardized, transparent, and digitally supported approach for assessing the quality, interpretability, and comparability of arbovirus surveillance data. The framework is designed to support researchers, public health practitioners, evidence-synthesis teams, and decision-makers in generating more robust, transparent, and context-sensitive evidence for surveillance, policy, and outbreak preparedness.
