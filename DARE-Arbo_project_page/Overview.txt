DARE-Arbo is a novel methodological framework developed to address a major challenge in arbovirus surveillance research. Studies often report biologically and methodologically different outcomes that are treated as if they were directly comparable. By using the article-outcome as the unit of appraisal, DARE-Arbo evaluates each estimate according to its target population, biological endpoint, assay pathway, verification process, and reporting quality. The framework bridges gaps left by existing prevalence, risk-of-bias, and diagnostic tools, providing a standardized, transparent, and digitally supported approach for interpreting and synthesizing arbovirus surveillance evidence.
