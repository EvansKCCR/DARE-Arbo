## Dissemination and Follow-Up

### 🌍 How will DARE-Arbo be shared?

The final DARE-Arbo framework will be disseminated through:

- ✅ Peer-reviewed publication
- ✅ Conference presentations and scientific meetings
- ✅ Open-access repositories (e.g., GitHub, Figshare, Zenodo)
- ✅ The DARE-Arbo Streamlit platform
- ✅ Professional and global health networks

Openly released resources will include the framework, scoring codebook, decision tree, implementation guide, and worked examples.

---

### 👥 What happens after you participate?

Collaborators will:

- ✅ Receive updates throughout framework development
- ✅ Receive a summary of pilot testing and reliability findings
- ✅ Review major revisions arising from the pilot phase
- ✅ Be informed of publications, presentations, and framework releases
- ✅ Receive access to the final framework package and supporting resources
- ✅ Be considered for authorship or acknowledgement based on contribution level
- ✅ Have opportunities to participate in future validation studies and framework updates

---

### 🎯 Why participate?

Help shape a next-generation framework for appraising arbovirus surveillance and seroepidemiological evidence while contributing to a globally accessible methodological resource for researchers, public health practitioners, and evidence-synthesis teams.
